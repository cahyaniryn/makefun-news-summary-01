Wednesday, 11 November 2026 — At COP31 in Antalya, UN Secretary-General António Guterres warned world leaders that the climate crisis was outpacing action and urged governments to tax oil companies’ windfall profits and end dependence on fossil fuels. AFP reported low expectations for a major breakthrough, with several prominent leaders absent from the summit. (AFP, via Yahoo News, 11 November.)

In the Democratic Republic of Congo, government data showed 11,042 confirmed Ebola cases and 5,387 deaths as of Tuesday, Reuters reported. The Bundibugyo virus outbreak, declared in mid-May, had affected eight of the country’s 26 provinces. Reuters noted that no approved treatments or vaccines existed for this species. (Reuters, republished by ETHealthworld, 11 November.)

A Florida federal judge allowed President Donald Trump’s $10 billion defamation suit against the BBC to proceed, but dismissed his separate claim under Florida’s Deceptive and Unfair Trade Practices Act. The case concerns the BBC’s editing of Trump’s 6 January 2021 speech. The judge ruled only that the defamation claim could continue, not whether it was proven. (BBC, 11 November.)
